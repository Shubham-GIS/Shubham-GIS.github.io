// ABOUT

Who I am

I'm a GIS Specialist with experience across geospatial data annotation, ELU/PLU mapping, and GIS quality assurance for both US-based geospatial projects and Indian municipal planning work. At Tejyog Infratech Consulting Engineers, I worked on Municipal Corporation planning projects for Nashik, Dhule, and Kolhapur — preparing Existing Land Use (ELU) and Proposed Land Use (PLU) datasets, digitizing planning maps, and coordinating a GIS team to keep deliverables on schedule.

I'm currently a GIS Specialist at Zuru Services, working on a US-based geospatial project on the client's ZeroMatter platform — annotating and classifying geospatial features, interpreting map imagery, and correcting inconsistencies in spatial datasets before delivery.

I hold a P.G. B.Sc. in GIS & Remote Sensing (Savitribai Phule Pune University) and a Master's in Geography (LVH College, Nashik).
